## 1. Shared frameworks (the vocabulary of "targets")

Audience/targeting decisions are expressed in three overlapping frameworks. Use these
as the dimensions when you segment people or define a program's target level.

### RANGE — behavioral AI fluency (how someone works with AI, not which tools)
| Letter | Dimension | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **R** | Reach | How far you push AI into new/unfamiliar territory |
| **A** | Autonomy | How independently you take work end-to-end |
| **N** | Navigation | How clearly you direct AI, orient in ambiguity, and know what to delegate |
| **G** | Generalization | How you transfer skills across contexts and enable others |
| **E** | Execution Fidelity | How consistently you ship quality, accountable output |

### Fluency levels — where a person is on the journey
`Pre-Pilot → Pilot → Builder → Multiplier`
- **Pre-Pilot:** occasional use; wins are real but inconsistent.
- **Pilot:** structured, repeatable AI work; output is measurably better. (A *maintained threshold*, not a finish line.)
- **Builder:** builds systems others depend on; they keep running when you step away.
- **Multiplier:** raises the AI capability of everyone around them.
> FY27 target for a typical BetterUpper: move **2–3 domain levels this year** (e.g., Pre-Pilot → Pilot in Execution Fidelity or Generalization), guided by the AI Fluency Assessment + manager.

### Adaptive Capacity — the coachable mindsets underneath (from the *Case for Conditions* research)
- **Curiosity** — play/tinker/experiment before you know the answer → maps to **Reach**.
- **Pilot mindset** — take the controls in ambiguity → maps to **Autonomy + Navigation**.
- **Courage** — make the harder call, ship, generalize → maps to **Generalization + Execution**.
> "Grow your RANGE and you're growing your Adaptive Capacity — same story, measured two ways."

## 2. Program catalog

### 2A. The three curated Coursera programs (FY27 Q3 focus)
Curated **with** Coursera for BetterUp. In Q3 these are the invited, focus programs;
the learning team defines the audiences that benefit most and invites specific groups.
They form an ordered on-ramp for anyone starting: **Expand Your RANGE → Running with Claude → Augment & Automate.**

---

#### Program 1 — Expand Your RANGE
- **Type:** Coursera program (self-paced) · **Format:** 3 short foundations
- **What it is:** Three foundations for AI fluency at BetterUp — *Rethink How You Decide with AI* · *Systems Thinking for AI & Automation* · *Trustworthy Generative AI*.
- **Audience / who it's for:** New BetterUppers and anyone still early on their AI journey. The recommended *first* step.
- **Prerequisite:** None. This is the entry point.
- **Moves you:** Pre-Pilot → Pilot (foundational).
- **RANGE emphasis:** Navigation, Reach.
- **Enrollment:** Open / invited by profile in Q3.
- **Link:** https://www.coursera.org/programs/range-oixf2?collectionId=CDg9T
- **Status:** ✅ live & linked (learning-coursera.html #q3, ai-acceleration.html, starting-at-betterup.html)

#### Program 2 — Running with Claude
- **Type:** Coursera program (self-paced) · **Format:** 3 courses
- **What it is:** From rough ideas to done right — *AI Fundamentals with Claude* · *AI Collaboration with Claude* · *AI Automation with Claude*.
- **Audience / who it's for:** People who've got the basics and want to elevate and optimize their everyday outputs with Claude. The second step after Expand Your RANGE.
- **Prerequisite:** Expand Your RANGE recommended first (not enforced).
- **Moves you:** Pilot → toward Builder.
- **RANGE emphasis:** Autonomy, Execution Fidelity.
- **Enrollment:** Open / invited by profile in Q3.
- **Link:** https://www.coursera.org/programs/nail-it-with-claude-8yhe3?collectionId=v18R9
- **Status:** ✅ live & linked

#### Program 3 — Augment & Automate Your BetterUp Processes
- **Type:** Coursera program (self-paced, hands-on build) · **Format:** build-your-own-workflow
- **What it is:** Turn a real, repeatable part of your own work into a mapped, automated, and eventually agentic workflow. Core sequence: **map before you automate — automate before you go agentic.**
- **Audience / who it's for:** BetterUppers ready to improve a real process they own.
- **Prerequisite:** Comfort with Claude (Running with Claude recommended).
- **Moves you:** Pilot → Builder.
- **RANGE emphasis:** Autonomy, Generalization, Execution Fidelity.
- **Enrollment:** Open / invited by profile in Q3.
- **Link:** https://coursera.org/programs/augment-and-automate-your-betterup-processes-ry3sb
- **Status:** ✅ live & linked

### 2B. Claude Foundations — coach-facilitated practice labs
Live, coach-facilitated labs (not self-paced, not lectures). Facilitated by BetterUp's
AI facilitators: **Alex Zesch** (AI Facilitator), **David Brinegar** (Data Architect),
**Lee Gonzales** (Director of Engineering). 🟡 headshot→name mapping to confirm.
Grouped on the hub as **"Claude Foundations"** (page: `claude-foundations.html`).

#### Program 4 — Claude Skills & Projects  ("Before you fly, you build.")
- **Type:** Live practice lab · **Format/Duration:** 2 hours, hands-on
- **What it is:** Stop treating Claude like a search bar; build persistent, reusable workflows using Claude Projects and Skills. Leave with a configured Project tied to your real job.
- **Audience / who it's for:** Employees already using Claude who find it useful but inconsistent and suspect they're using ~30% of it. Not intimidated by tech — just haven't had structured time to go deeper.
- **Prerequisite:** Some prior Claude use.
- **Moves you:** Pre-Pilot/Pilot → Pilot (repeatable practice).
- **Learning objectives:** configure a Project + write a system prompt; activate ≥2 Skills mapped to their role; audit usage to find repeated manual prompts; compare unstructured vs Project-backed output.
- **Enrollment:** 🟡 registration URL TBD (recordings lobby; a July 15 2026 cohort has ended, recordings available until Jul 14 2027).
- **Status:** ✅ page live; registration link pending.

#### Program 5 — Claude Cowork  ("Don't prompt it. Deploy it.")
- **Type:** Live practice lab · **Format/Duration:** 2 hours, hands-on
- **What it is:** The shift from Claude *responding* to Claude *acting*. Give Claude access to your real environment (files, tools, systems) and build a working Cowork workflow against a recurring task. Develop judgment about human-in-the-loop review vs. autonomous run.
- **Audience / who it's for:** People who completed **Skills & Projects**, still do too much manual work, and have ≥1 recurring multi-tool workflow. Comfortable connecting Claude to their tools; need structure + a safe space.
- **Prerequisite:** Claude Skills & Projects.
- **Moves you:** Pilot → Builder.
- **RANGE emphasis:** Autonomy, Navigation, Execution Fidelity.
- **Enrollment:** 🟡 "Register your interest" URL TBD.
- **Status:** ✅ page live; registration link pending.

### 2C. AFS Next — the Build Lab
#### Program 6 — AFS Next  ("Build what keeps running.")
- **Type:** Mentored, coached **build lab** (not a training program) · **Format:** 3 live working sessions + guided build time between them; virtual; small cohort; paired with a "wingman," coached by a facilitator-as-spotter.
- **What it is:** Take one real, high-leverage team problem and **ship a working AI system** against it (a Claude Project, a Skill, an agent). You leave with something deployed that other people use — not a certificate. *"AFS 100 taught you to fly. AFS Next is where you build the aircraft others fly."*
- **The work moves:** **Map → Build → Prove.** (S1 Map: find the leverage / a real target. S2 Build: ship the system. S3 Prove: hand it off, watch it run without you, get certified on adoption evidence.)
- **Audience / who it's for:** Builders past basic prompting who want to *own* a system, have a real team-level problem they're tired of solving by hand, and want impact beyond their own to-do list — **plus the managers who release them** (protect a few hours/week of build time).
- **Prerequisite:** **AFS 100** (🟡 see open questions — AFS = AI Flight School lineage; AI Flight School itself is retired, so confirm what "AFS 100" currently maps to).
- **Moves you:** Builder → Multiplier.
- **RANGE emphasis:** all five; explicitly framed as the RANGE ↔ Adaptive Capacity capstone.
- **Signature standard:** *The Still Running Test* — "If you left tomorrow, what would still be running that others depend on?"
- **Outcomes:** deployed AI system (named users); force-multiplication evidence; RANGE trajectory; Builder Badge readiness; a 90-Day Flight Plan; grown Adaptive Capacity.
- **Enrollment:** No form — email **bu.learning@betterup.co** to join the next cohort (or manager nomination to same address).
- **Status:** ✅ page live (`afs-next.html`, built from Fabia's one-pager).

## 4. Audience segments & invitation mechanisms

**Segments to target (draft — refine in the new repo):**
1. **New hires / earliest-stage** → Starting at BetterUp, Expand Your RANGE.
2. **Individual contributors improving daily output** → Running with Claude, Skills & Projects.
3. **Process owners** (a repeatable workflow they own) → Augment & Automate, Cowork.
4. **Builders** (ready to ship systems others rely on) → AFS Next.
5. **People managers / leaders** → AI Acceleration for Managers (+ everything above for themselves).

**Invitation mechanisms in play (how targeting actually happens):**
- **Invited by profile** — Q3 Coursera trio; the learning team defines who benefits most and invites specific groups.
- **Selected / critical roles + manager nomination** — AFS Next.
- **Sequential prerequisite** — Cowork requires Skills & Projects; AFS Next requires AFS 100.
- **Open / self-serve** — anyone can start the Coursera trio; request a license via bu.learning@betterup.co if not yet invited.
