What This Is

A development map,
not a performance tool.

AI Fluency at BetterUp is the ability to work with AI as a genuine collaborator — knowing when to bring it in, how to direct it clearly, how to evaluate what it gives you, and how to take full ownership of what you do with it.

This framework defines what fluency looks like in practice, maps where you stand across 5 RANGE dimensions, and connects gaps to specific learning resources.

✦
Iterative by nature
85.7% of effective AI interactions involve multiple refinement cycles. If your prompts never change, your fluency isn't growing.
✦
Non-linear growth
You can be at Pilot in Judgment and Pre-Pilot in Workflow Design. That's not a problem — it's the point.
✦
Evidence, not aspiration
Your level is what you do consistently and can demonstrate — not what you've completed or intend to do.
Three practices — what type of AI work are you doing?

Three Practices of
AI Collaboration

You don't graduate from one to another. All three exist at every level. What changes is the complexity and stakes of the tasks you apply them to.

Practice 01
Automate

AI completes a task on your behalf. You define it; AI executes it. You hand over the work — you keep the accountability.

↳ AI drafts a weekly recap from your meeting notes
Practice 02
Augment

You and AI work side by side. You stay in the driver's seat. AI extends your reach — your judgment governs every decision.

↳ You write a proposal; AI stress-tests your logic and surfaces gaps
Practice 03
Agentic

AI acts more independently within guardrails you define. You review outcomes. Trust is delegated — not abdicated.

↳ AI monitors a pipeline and flags anomalies; you decide what to do

Pre-Pilot
"I've started, but my work hasn't really changed yet."

AI is a convenience. Usage is ad hoc. You can't point to clear evidence that your output is meaningfully different as a result of AI. You're building awareness and early habits.

Watch For
One-off prompts. Unedited outputs. No before/after story. Uncertainty about which tool to use and why.
Pilot
"I use AI to operate at a meaningfully higher level."

You have a structured, repeatable approach. You can demonstrate your AI workflow and explain where you trust it and where you don't. Your output is concretely better — not just faster.

The Test
Can you tell your AI growth story across three points in time — what you started with, what changed, and why?
Builder
"I orchestrate AI and build things others depend on."

You build reusable systems. Your team stopped doing something manually because of what you built. You treat your AI setup like a product — it has versions, it gets updated, and others use it.

The Test
If you disappeared tomorrow, would what you built still be running? Do 2+ people depend on it?
Multiplier
"I reshape how work happens — not just how fast it moves."

You've changed what work exists. Categories of tasks no longer require human effort. You've influenced how the broader organization operates. AI handles execution; you own judgment, strategy, and design.

The Test
Can you point to something your team or org stopped doing entirely? Have others adopted a way of working you introduced?
